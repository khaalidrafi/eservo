* Bugs found while landing the adb unit (worth re-reading)

All three were caught by the adb ERT tests, fixed, and pinned by
tests; they are classic Elisp traps, so the fixes carry comments:

1. Match-data clobbering in =eservo-adb--parse-nodes= (eservo-adb.el):
   helper =string-match= calls destroyed the outer loop's
   =(match-end 0)=, freezing the scan position -> infinite loop (the
   whole suite hung).  Fix: capture tag+end FIRST.
2. Empty-string truthiness: uiautomator writes =text=""= on most
   nodes; =(or "" fallback)= never falls back, so content-desc labels
   were dropped.  Fix: =eservo-adb--xml-attr-nonempty=.
3. =(apply #'eservo-adb--tap driver (center))= spread the (X Y) pair
   into two arguments -> wrong-number-of-arguments.  Fix: pass the
   pair as ONE list argument.
